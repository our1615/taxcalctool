Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 29, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Tax Calc Tool is designed with privacy as a core principle. Our calculators operate entirely within your web browser using JavaScript. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal tax information you enter into our calculators.

2. Automatic Data Collection

Like most websites, we may collect standard server logs including your IP address, browser type, referring pages, and timestamps. This data is used for security and analytics purposes only and is not linked to any personal tax information.

3. Cookies

We use essential cookies to remember your display preferences. Third-party advertisers may use cookies to serve relevant ads. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

4. Third-Party Services

We use Google Analytics to understand site usage patterns. We display advertisements from third-party networks. These services may collect data according to their own privacy policies.

5. Data Security

Since no personal tax data leaves your device, the primary security risk is standard web browsing. We use HTTPS encryption for all pages.

6. Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy periodically. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact privacy@taxcalctool.org.
