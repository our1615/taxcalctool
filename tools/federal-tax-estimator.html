How It Works

1. What This Calculator Does

This estimator calculates your 2026 federal income tax using the OBBBA-adjusted tax brackets. It applies the standard deduction or your entered itemized deductions, then calculates tax owed at each bracket rate.

2. When to Use It

Use this calculator when planning your tax withholding, evaluating a job offer, or estimating quarterly payments. It's especially useful in Q4 when adjusting W-4 withholdings before year-end.

3. Calculation Method

Taxable Income = Gross Income - Pre-Tax Deductions - Greater of(Standard Deduction, Itemized Deductions). Tax is calculated progressively: 10% on first bracket, 12% on next, etc. Credits subtracted at the end.

4. Understanding Results

The effective rate shows your average tax burden. A 15% effective rate on $80k income means you keep $68k after federal tax. Compare this to your withholding to see if you'll owe or get a refund.

5. Disclaimer

This is an estimate for planning only. Actual tax depends on many factors including AMT, additional Medicare tax, capital gains, and specific credits. Consult a CPA for your exact liability.
