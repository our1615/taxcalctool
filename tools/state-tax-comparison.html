    How It Works

    1. What This Calculator Does

    Compares state income tax burdens across all 50 states based on your income level. Shows estimated tax for each state using current 2026 rates and brackets. Select any two states for a side-by-side comparison with dollar savings.

    2. When to Use It

    Use this when considering a move, evaluating a remote work arrangement, or comparing job offers in different states. Also useful for retirees choosing where to settle. Remember: state tax is just one factor in cost of living.

    3. Calculation Method

    For flat-tax states: Income × flat rate. For progressive states: applies brackets progressively like federal tax. No-tax states show $0. Estimates are for state income tax only - does not include property tax, sales tax, or local taxes.

    4. Understanding Results

    The dollar difference between two states is your annual savings (or cost) from moving. A $5,000 difference on $75k income is significant. But factor in property taxes, cost of living, and quality of life. Texas has no income tax but high property taxes; California has high income tax but Prop 13 limits property tax growth.

    5. Disclaimer

    State tax rates change frequently. This tool uses 2026 rates as of May 2026. Some states have local income taxes not included. Does not account for deductions, credits, or special rules (e.g., NH/TX dividend taxes). Verify current rates with state revenue departments.
