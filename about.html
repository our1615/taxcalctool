Why I Created This Site

After 12 years inside the IRS, I saw the same pattern over and over: regular people terrified of the tax code, overpaying because they didn't understand deductions, or getting hit with penalties because they missed quarterly payments. The tax code is complex, but it shouldn't be scary.

I left the IRS in 2020 to build tools that make taxes transparent. Every calculator on this site is tested against official IRS worksheets. Every article is written from the perspective of someone who's sat on both sides of the desk.

I live in Austin, Texas — a state with no income tax, which gives me a unique perspective on federal vs state tax planning. When I'm not reading tax court cases for fun, you'll find me smoking brisket, kayaking Lady Bird Lake, or arguing about Longhorns football.
